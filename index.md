I am a combined B.S./M.S.E. student in Computer Science at **Johns Hopkins
University**, where I work with
[Prof. Homanga Bharadhwaj](https://homangab.github.io/) and
[Prof. Anand Bhattad](https://anandbhattad.github.io/). I am also currently a
Research Intern at the
[Harvard Kempner Institute](https://kempnerinstitute.harvard.edu/), working with
[Prof. Qianqian Wang](https://qianqianwang68.github.io/) and
[Prof. Yilun Du](https://yilundu.github.io/).

I am interested in how machines can understand the **dynamics of the physical
world** — how scenes are structured, how things move, and how they interact.
I am especially interested in how this understanding can support robots that
perceive, predict, and act in the real world.
